🏠 Sea Breeze Homestay

Rooms & Descriptions

Nestled 50 metres from Velugu Cove beach, Sea Breeze Homestay is a welcoming family-run guesthouse hosted by Lakshmi. Choose from breezy Standard Double rooms (₹1,500/night), Deluxe Sea View rooms (₹2,200/night), or spacious 4-guest Family Suites (₹3,000/night) — all including a freshly prepared South Indian breakfast.

Amenities

  • 🏖️ Beach Access — 50 metres to Velugu Cove shore

⛵ Cove Boat Tours

⚠️ Safety Information

Every passenger is provided a fitted life jacket that must be worn throughout the trip. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult. Meena's crew performs morning weather and sea-condition checks before every launch and cancels or reschedules if seas are rough.
